Information Security Policy
---------------------------
Acme Corporation strives to keep its systems secure. This policy is intended to promote awareness of security practices.

Access Control Policy
---------------------
Users are provided access based on requests from their team leads.
User access is sometimes reviewed.
Passwords should be secure.
MFA is optional but encouraged for remote access.

Asset Management Policy
-----------------------
Assets are sometimes tracked.
Confidential information must be handled properly.

Cryptography Policy
-------------------
Encryption should be used wherever possible.
TLS is recommended for web traffic.

Backup and Recovery Policy
--------------------------
Systems are backed up periodically.
Backups are stored safely.

Incident Management Policy
--------------------------
Serious security issues must be reported to IT.
Post-incident reviews may be conducted for certain incidents.

Physical Security Policy
------------------------
Visitors are allowed into restricted areas if accompanied.
Some access is logged.
CCTV may be in place in some areas.

Supplier Management Policy
--------------------------
Vendor contracts are signed.
Security is expected from all vendors.

Change Management Policy
------------------------
Changes should be tested before going live.
Emergency changes are handled quickly.

Compliance Policy
-----------------
We intend to align with ISO/IEC 27001.
Audits may be conducted from time to time.
